Your AI agent, told when new roles match.

reqwatch is an open-source MCP server that reads employers' own job boards and tells your agent about new openings that fit your interests, after checking each one is still live.

Starting with US new-grad and intern tech roles, with sponsorship and graduation-window filters.

Self-host free

One email when hosted opens. Nothing else.

How it works

  1. 01

    Set your interests

    Choose roles, locations, whether you need visa sponsorship, and your graduation window.

  2. 02

    Your agent calls list_matches

    Any MCP client can ask what's new since it last looked. No special integration needed.

  3. 03

    Get verified-live roles

    Results come straight from Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby boards, and each role is re-checked as still open.

Honest status

What works today: the list_matches tool works with every MCP client.

Push notifications are an upgrade, not the foundation: most clients don't surface them yet, so don't count on being interrupted. Ask your agent, and it will tell you what's new.
